Break veins open rather than emptying them	打破礦脈而非直接取空
Breaking drives the game's own state machine at the vein, the swing landing and then the break, so the node spills its contents on the ground and disappears exactly as it does under a pickaxe. Emptying instead lifts the ore straight out and skips the drop entirely. Neither route pays your tool's Mining Yield Up, which the game only grants to a swing it counts as yours, so set the extra below to make up the difference. Each vein is struck once and then left alone until the game respawns it.	打破礦脈會驅動遊戲自身在該礦脈的狀態機，也就是揮擊命中後接著碎裂，讓資源點把內容物灑落在地上並消失，與用鎬子敲開時完全相同。取空則是直接把礦石取出，完全略過掉落過程。兩種方式都不會套用工具的「採礦收穫加成」，因為遊戲只會把它給予算作你自己的一次揮擊，所以請用下方的額外礦石來補足差額。每條礦脈只會被打一次，之後便會保持原狀，直到遊戲讓它重生為止。
