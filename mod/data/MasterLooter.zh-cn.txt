Break veins open rather than emptying them	击碎矿脉而非直接清空
Breaking drives the game's own state machine at the vein, the swing landing and then the break, so the node spills its contents on the ground and disappears exactly as it does under a pickaxe. Emptying instead lifts the ore straight out and skips the drop entirely. Neither route pays your tool's Mining Yield Up, which the game only grants to a swing it counts as yours, so set the extra below to make up the difference. Each vein is struck once and then left alone until the game respawns it.	击碎会驱动矿脉自身的游戏状态机：先是挥击命中，再是击碎，因此资源点会将内容物洒落在地面上并消失，与被镐子击碎时完全一样。直接清空则会将矿石直接取出，完全跳过掉落过程。两种方式都不会触发你工具的“挖矿产出提升”，因为游戏只会将其判定为算你自己的挥击才给予加成，所以请用下方的“额外”选项来补足这部分差额。每条矿脉只会被击中一次，随后保持不变，直到游戏将其重新生成。
