For Crimson Desert 2.01.00 (exe 1.0.0.2760).

Weapons you throw stay yours, unchecking Furniture now covers the room rather
than the tables, and lizards can be caught.

## Thrown weapons were duplicating

Throw a weapon with the Weapon Throw skill and the mod would pick it up off the
ground while the game was still returning it to your hand. You ended up holding
two. It only happened sometimes, because it was a race between the two.

Being attached to you is the one thing that keeps your own gear out of the loot
engine, and a weapon in the air is not attached to anything. That is exactly the
window in which it is lying in the world and within reach. Nothing further down
caught it either, since a thrown sword is the same weapon class as one off a
corpse.

The mod now notes what is hanging off you as it goes past, and refuses anything
that was on you in the last forty-five seconds. The entity and the item have to
agree before it refuses, because the game recycles entity ids and a stale
refusal would otherwise land on something innocent. Anything else you drop on
purpose is left alone for the same three quarters of a minute, which it should
have been all along.

Found by lsimo.

## Furniture covered the tables and nothing else in the room

Candles and lamps were still being taken with the Furniture box unchecked.

1.3.0 moved that switch off a prefab name and onto the item's class, which
caught the camp tables that started the complaint. The class turns out to be a
single label chosen by priority, and "furniture" sits twelfth of the thirteen
furnishing tags in that order. So a candelabra files as a light, a glass lamp as
a lamp, a still life as a painting and a pot as a flower pot, and not one of
them ever reached the line that read the class.

It reads the tag now. That is 452 pieces where it was 123: 78 paintings, 60
dyes, 46 lamps, 42 ornaments, 34 flower pots, 30 cooking facilities, 26 lights
and 13 decorations. Chests, goblets and anything else that holds something stay
with the Containers switch, where they already were.

Found by Fyreon87.

## Lizards could never be caught

Chameleons, iguanas and the two lizards were never picked up, whatever the
switches said. They were not reaching the switches at all.

What can be caught alive is decided from bytes the game hangs off a creature,
and the test insisted on a combination that ground lizards do not wear. An
iguana carries a different one, so it failed outright. The test also demanded a
second byte that turns out to separate nothing: the same insect appears under
both values, three metres apart in one session, and the ones wearing the wrong
value were being refused in silence.

Both are gone. What keeps people and livestock out of this is the category
byte, and that is all it reads now. Lizards are held to a stricter bar than the
insects, since their byte is the least mapped of the three: the creature table
has to name the animal outright, and the animal has to be something that becomes
an item.

Worth knowing before you report it. Birds are filed as animals, so turning Small
animals on for the lizards will also start catching pigeons and crows.

Found by LuxDragon.

## Also

The mod used to identify only a creature it had already decided it could catch,
so anything it refused was logged as "creature" with no name. Across forty-one
logs that is 452 refused creatures and not one of them named, which is why the
lizards went undiagnosed for two days. Live creatures are named now whether they
are caught or not.

Asking the game whether taking something counts as stealing is a call into the
game, and it sat at the end of a decision that runs thirty times a second on
everything in range. One object was asked thirty-four times in a second and a
tenth. The answer is remembered for two seconds now, and dropped whenever the
player changes.

The keys have always been in MasterLooter.ini beside the plugin, as MenuKey,
KeyToggle, KeyBurst and KeyWatch, and that was written down nowhere. It is in
the description now, along with the reason a menu sometimes opens and will not
take a click: in borderless windowed the Windows pointer and the game's pointer
drift apart, and moving to the top left corner once puts them back together.
Asked by Torsten and Brizius, answered by LuxDragon.

The translation template was missing everything 1.4.0 added, so a translator
could not see the Crops switch or the rewritten ore and plants text. Rebuilt.
