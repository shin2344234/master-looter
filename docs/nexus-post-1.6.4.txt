[size=4][b]1.6.4 fixes two crashes, and you only reported one of them[/b][/size]

The one you reported is lsimo's. They fast travelled, the game died twenty-four
minutes into a normal session, and they pulled the crash dump apart themselves
before I got near it: a call through a null function pointer with my plugin on
the stack. That saved me a day.

Here is what it was. The mod cannot do everything on its own thread, so it
queues work up for the game's thread to run, and each queued job holds a pointer
to the thing it is aimed at. A teleport throws that entire world away and builds
a new one. The queue had no idea, so four seconds after the jump it went ahead
and drove a state change at a rock that no longer existed.

Anything queued is now dropped the moment the world changes. If it ever happens
again the log will say how many jobs were thrown away at each jump, which is the
first thing I would want to look at.

Then I went looking for the same mistake elsewhere and found it. Winding a well
is eleven seconds of timed steps, and it was holding the same kind of pointer
across all of them, outside the queue where the first fix could reach. Fast
travel in the middle of drawing water and it kept going, at objects that were
gone. That run gets abandoned now.

[size=4][b]The one nobody reported[/b][/size]

Hooking a game function means writing a redirect over its first instructions and
keeping the originals somewhere safe. I was doing those two things in the wrong
order: the redirect went in first, and only afterwards did the mod write down
where the original had gone. Every redirect calls the original immediately.

So there was a gap, a few instructions wide, where a game thread could arrive
and call through nothing at all. Narrow, but these hooks go in while the world
is loading and one of them sits on a function that runs thousands of times a
second. From the outside it would look like the game dying on startup for no
reason, with a log that stops mid sentence.

I do not know whether anyone has hit it. Nobody could have reported it usefully
if they had.

[size=4][b]Damiane and Oongka, again[/b][/size]

Cmz4455 reported no auto looting on those two, with a log, which is the only
reason this is fixed. The scan had wandered off the player and settled on a
steam engine. After that every part of the train read as the player's own kit
and got skipped, so nothing anywhere near them was picked up.

The mod scores candidates and a dressed character ought to beat a train without
trying. It lost on a technicality: the train dragged enough separate objects
into range to push the player out of a list that only holds thirty-two, and once
the player is not in the list, none of the rules I wrote to stop exactly this
apply to anything. Whatever the scan is standing on cannot be pushed out of that
list any more, and a thing whose parts have never once been recognised as items
cannot take the centre from a thing whose have.

[size=4][b]Two you asked for[/b][/size]

LuxDragon asked, back when ground lizards became catchable, whether specific
creatures could be filtered. There is a Creatures tab now. Search a name, set it
to always or never, and it wins over the Insects, Fish and Small animals
switches. Ten catchable insects have nothing in the game's item data behind
them, so naming the creature is the only way to refuse them.

Ahplla asked for a key for taking owned goods. It is under General, Keys, and it
is unbound until you set one, along with a pad chord. That switch earns you a
bounty while it is on, so it wants flipping for a few seconds and flipping back,
and digging through a menu twice is not that.

[size=4][b]Also[/b][/size]

Drawing a well was quietly opening the extra-ore window, so a vein you mined by
hand during one collected the tool bonus. That is the single thing the setting
promises will not happen.

An old settings file was being migrated on every launch instead of once, because
the migration was never actually saved, which reverted a hand edit every start
and slowly pushed your real backups out of the list.

Presets and restored backups change the menu language now, instead of changing
the setting and leaving the menu in English.

The log names whichever mod hooked DirectX before this one, rather than telling
you only that something did. Faults are labelled first chance with a note saying
most of them are the game probing memory on purpose, because people paste them
to me as the crash and they usually are not.

And a lookup the scan leans on had been giving up about five seconds into every
session and doing the slow thing from then on. It does not any more.
