For Crimson Desert 2.01.00 (exe 1.0.0.2760).

Auto-loot works as Damiane and Oongka. Kliff does not need to be in the party.

## Playing as anyone

Every auto-loot mod for this game, this one included, centred its search on
the player-tagged actor. As Kliff that is you. As Damiane or Oongka it is not:
the game keeps your identity and your body as two separate entities, and the
identity sits at a fixed spot with nothing within a kilometre of it while your
body is off in the world wearing your armour. So the mod scanned forty metres
of empty ground 1,700 metres from you and found nothing, which is the whole of
"auto-loot only works with Kliff".

The body is found by its gear now. A dressed character is the one thing with
a handful of items attached to it, and one of them is enough to say where you
stand. The moment the identity proves to be standing nowhere, the search moves
onto the body carrying the most on your own route. Loot events still go out
under your identity, which is what the game itself does, so nothing else
changed.

It took eight attempts because the game hands over its list of objects a few
at a time, and every earlier rule decided from a single pass. The fix remembers
what was seen over fifteen seconds so the pieces add up. The five rules that
failed are written down so nobody writes them again:
https://github.com/shin2344234/master-looter/blob/main/docs/ore-investigation/DAMIANE.md

Confirmed as Damiane in a lavender field that had given nothing all afternoon,
and as Oongka.

## Known

A wagon carries ten attached parts and a horse carries tack, both usually
beside you, and the mod can briefly pick one of them as the body. It works
because they are beside you; a wagon fifty metres off pulls the search with it
for a moment. Fix to follow.

## Also

The log now says where the scan is standing and why: how many objects the
game handed over, how many were in range, the nearest one, who is carrying
gear and on which route, and every time the centre moves. All of it only under
the debug log.
