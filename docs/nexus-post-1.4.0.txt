1.4.0 is up. The big change is how ore veins get mined.

Until now the mod sent the game a drop event at the vein. The ore spilled out and
you picked it up, but the node itself stayed standing, so the same vein could be
struck again on a later pass. That is gone. The mod now fires the two events your
pickaxe fires, the swing landing and then the break, and lets the game's own
state machine take it from there. The vein empties itself and disappears, the way
it does when you swing at it.

Because that replaced the old path instead of sitting next to it, the three
checkboxes for breaking ore are one again. Both experiments are gone.

One thing worth knowing before you report it as a bug. A vein pays either one or
two ore, and which you get depends on whether your character is carrying a mining
bonus buff at that moment: two of the node's three drop rows are gated on it.
Mining by hand behaves exactly the same way, so this is the game's own rule and
not something the mod is costing you. The yield is simply not constant, and you
will watch it change during a run.

That one ate the whole day. I mapped the drop path, found the function that
decides how many items a broken node spawns, and pulled the condition text
straight out of the game's own tables. Three separate fixes looked right, got
built, got tested, and did nothing. What finally settled it was a log showing
fourteen mod-driven breaks in a row paying the full two ore, and then dropping to
one on their own with nothing changed. It was never mine to fix. Some of the day
was wasted, but the map is written down now and the next problem in there will be
much cheaper.

Also in this release:

Ice walls and other puzzle objects are no longer struck. Five of them are tagged
as ore in the game's own data and the mod was taking that at face value.

Chinese, Japanese and Korean menus render properly. They used to come out as
boxes.

Logs rotate. The last twelve sessions are kept and compressed, so a report can
include the run before the one that broke.

Crops have their own switch, separate from plants.

Checksums and the VirusTotal report are in the description, and the full source
is on GitHub. Thanks to everyone who posted logs; almost everything fixed here
was found from one.
