[size=4][b]1.6.3: your pickaxe finally counts, and an apology about carcasses[/b][/size]

Two things, and one of them is me correcting something the mod page has been
telling you that was not true.

[size=4][b]The ore[/b][/size]

Break a vein by hand with a refined drill and you get three. Let the mod break
the same vein and you get one. Someone was going to notice eventually, and the
answer took eleven builds.

The mod does not swing a pickaxe. It drives the vein's own break sequence, which
is the whole reason the node spills and disappears exactly the way it does under
a real swing rather than evaporating in your hand. It turns out the game walks
two drop rows for a swing it counts as yours, and one for this.

I found the function that decides the number and put a log line on it, then
mined by hand and let the mod mine, and compared. Every single input that
function reads is identical between the two. Same instigator, same vein, same
key. So it is not doing smaller arithmetic for the mod. There is a second row it
simply never reaches.

Where that row comes from I still do not know, and I would rather say that than
invent a reason.

So the fix is blunt. There is a new slider under Reaching nodes, Extra ore per
vein, and the ore it adds goes out through the game's own drop path, so it lands
on the ground with the rest of what the vein spills. Nothing gets copied into
your bag and nothing touches your save.

Set it to what your tool pays by hand, minus one. If your drill gives three,
put it on 2. It only applies to a vein this mod broke, so mining by hand is
untouched and the two cannot stack. It is off unless you turn it on, and you can
move it mid-session without restarting.

[size=4][b]The carcasses[/b][/size]

This part is a correction, not a feature.

The description said every item is checked against the item database. For
skinning a carcass that has never been true, and it cannot be made true. A
carcass carries nothing the mod can read. The event that skins it names the
carcass and nothing else, the yield never exists as anything the mod could look
at, and once the game hands you an item there is no way to give it back.

So your class rules, your tag rules, your per-item list, the minimum value and
Skip quest items have all been sitting there not applying to skinning, while the
page told you they did. The Carcasses switch is the only control and always was.
On skins everything, off skins nothing. That is what the description, the README
and the menu say now.

Human corpses were never part of this. Their gear lands on the ground as
ordinary items and gets the full check like anything else.

There was a real bug hiding next to it, and that one is fixed. Anything the game
had flagged as dead went down the skinning path before the mod checked whether
it was carrying ordinary item data, so a dead thing that did have an identity
lost every rule on the way past. It goes through the same filter as everything
else now.

If you have been relying on Skip quest items while skinning, nothing has changed
in what the mod does. What changed is that it now tells you the truth about it.
