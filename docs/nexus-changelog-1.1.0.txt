1.1.0 (2026-09-06), for Crimson Desert 2.01.00 (exe 1.0.0.2760)

Plants, ore, stone and wood are collected on a fresh install. They were not before.

- Gather nodes are now identified by the prefab they were placed from, matched against a table of 1,042 node kinds built from the game's own data and compiled into the plugin. Previously a node was only known once you had gathered one of its kind by hand, so on a new install every plant and every ore vein was skipped as unidentified. Thanks to LuxDragon for the report and the log that made this findable.
- The number a node reports for itself is not the same from one session to the next. A remembered pairing could therefore file a node under the wrong switch: on one machine the id that meant Shrubby Sophora one evening meant a sweet potato patch the next. Nothing is remembered after you quit any more, MasterLooter.learned.tsv is deleted, and what the mod works out from your bag now lasts only the session.
- Presets. Every setting and every class, tag and item rule saved under a name in a MasterLooter.presets folder, and swapped in two clicks from the General tab. Copy the folder to keep your presets across a reinstall.
- A backup of your settings, written once each time the game starts before anything can change them, with a button to restore it. A version that changes a default, or an afternoon of fiddling, is one click to undo. A migration also leaves a copy stamped with the version it came from.
- A notice when the bag stops taking things. The game does not announce a full bag, so the mod watches what happens after each pick-up: five sent in a row with nothing arriving raises it, and anything landing clears it. Turn it off under General.
- The Nearby tab and the log name a node instead of showing a number.
- A node whose id happened to match an item row could be treated as that item. Gather ids and item ids are now kept apart.
