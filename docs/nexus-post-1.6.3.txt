[size=4][b]1.6.3 makes your mining tool count[/b][/size]

Mine an iron vein by hand with a refined drill and you get three ore. The mod,
working the same vein, brings back one. That has been true since veins went in.

The mod never swings anything. It drives the vein's own break sequence, which is
why the node spills and vanishes the way it does under a real pickaxe instead of
evaporating out from under you. Somewhere in that, the game decides to walk two
drop rows for your swing and one for the mod's.

I put a log line on the function that picks the number, mined a vein by hand,
then let the mod take the next one. Every value that function reads is the same
in both cases. Same arithmetic, one fewer row.

Eleven builds and I still cannot tell you where that second row comes from.

So this is the crude version. Reaching nodes has a slider now, Extra ore per
vein. Whatever you set goes out through the game's own drop path, so it spills
on the ground with the rest of the vein rather than appearing in your bag. Set it
to what your tool pays by hand minus one, so a drill that gives you three wants
2. Veins you break yourself are untouched and the two never stack. Off unless you
turn it on, and you can move it while you play.

[size=4][b]About carcasses[/b][/size]

The mod page has been telling you something that is not true. Skinning a carcass
ignores every filter you have set: class rules, tag rules, your per-item list,
the minimum value, Skip quest items. None of them have ever applied there.

I cannot make them apply. A carcass carries nothing the mod is able to read, and
the event that skins it names the carcass without saying what comes out of it. By
the time anything exists to be judged, the game has already handed it to you.

The Carcasses switch has been the only real control the whole time. The
description, the README and the menu say that now.

Human corpses are a separate thing and were never involved. Their gear drops on
the ground as ordinary items and gets the full check.

One real bug did turn up next door and is fixed. Anything the game had flagged as
dead went down the skinning path before the mod looked at whether it was carrying
normal item data, so a dead thing that did have an identity got past every rule.
That goes through the same check as everything else now.

If you had Skip quest items on and assumed it covered skinning, turn Carcasses
off.
