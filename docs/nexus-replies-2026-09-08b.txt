[b]lsimo[/b] Bag totals rather than the number on screen is exactly what I
needed, and it closes out a question left over from the 1.3.0 reports. Your
message covers separate ground in each paragraph, so I will take them as they
came.

[b]The multiplier is fine. The tool is what goes missing.[/b] Five is one times
five, so your multiplier is scaling mod-mined ore correctly. The old report of
x1 was written against 1.3.0 and 1.4.0 changed how veins get mined completely.

You explained the real problem yourself without noticing. You said the
Knuckledrill puts ore into your inventory instead of on the floor. That is a
separate path and the game runs it off a real weapon swing. The mod never
swings anything: it drives the vein's own break sequence, the ore spills on the
ground, and it collects that. You get the spill, times five, and the tool's
share never happens.

There is a comment in my own source claiming that breaking a vein is the one
way to get the tool bonus. I wrote it and never checked it. Your numbers
show it is wrong. Issue #31, and that comment comes out either way.

[b]A built-in multiplier per class.[/b] Fair ask, and most of the machinery is
already there because every rule in the mod is keyed per class, per tag or per
item. That is #28. One caution before I build it: multiplying loot means
sending the same take more than once, and duplicate items are how saves get
corrupted. I would rather be slow on that one.

[b]Pets.[/b] The mod is not doing it. There is no pet code in it anywhere, and
everything the mod takes goes through the full filter before it goes anywhere.
So the pet ignoring your settings is the game.

Whether I could stop it is a better question, and I gave you a worse answer to
it than you deserved. Since the mod watches every event the game raises, and
sits at the point where one could be dropped before the game acts on it, the
machinery is closer to hand than I first said. Two things decide whether it
works. A pet picking something up might raise no event at all, in which case
there is nothing to catch. Or it might raise one stamped with you rather than
with the pet, in which case I cannot tell it apart from your own pickups and I
would be guessing with your inventory.

One capture settles both, and you can do it on the build you have. Turn the
debug log on under General, let your pet take a few things, quit, and send me
MasterLooter.log from bin64. There is a diagnostic in there already that
records events the game raises about things other than you, which is the shape
this would be. That blind spot has fooled me twice before, on ore veins and on
wells, where an empty log looked like proof nothing existed and turned out to
be proof of a filter. So I would rather look than assume.

REPLY 3 -- to jacksterson, Proton
=============================================================================

[b]jacksterson[/b] No log file at all settles more than you might expect. The
log is opened the moment the DLL loads, well before anything gets drawn, so a
plugin that started and then died still leaves the file behind. Yours never
reached that point.

Which means the mod is not what is stopping your game. Something further
upstream is keeping it from being loaded at all, and that is a different
problem with a different fix.

Your overrides look right to me. winmm=n,b is the one that matters and you have
it. Two things I would check, in this order:

[b]1.[/b] Is MasterLooter.asi sitting in bin64 inside the prefix, next to
winmm.dll? A mod manager working through a Windows-shaped path can end up
writing somewhere the prefix never looks.

[b]2.[/b] Does the game start with winmm.dll present and MasterLooter.asi
deleted? That tells us whether the loader is unhappy with your configuration
generally or whether this plugin specifically is what kills it. If the game is
fine without the .asi and dies with it, then the plugin is getting far enough
to take the process down before it can open its own log, and I would start
looking at the byte pattern scan or at the Direct3D hook meeting DXVK instead
of real D3D12.

One thing I noticed: you are overriding dxgi as well, for RenoDX. This mod
hooks the DXGI present path too. Two things reaching for it under DXVK is a
plausible way to lose a process without a word, though I want to be clear that
is where I would look next and not a diagnosis.

There is no Linux machine here, so I am relying on you for anything past this.
Issue #15 has everything so far.

REPLY 4 -- to LuxDragon, filtering individual creatures
=============================================================================

[b]LuxDragon[/b] This is #29 now, so it is out of my head and somewhere I will
actually see it.

Where it really stands: items already answer to three levels of control, per
item, per tag and per class. Creatures answer to none of them. They get
recognised by their model and sorted by a pair of category bytes, so unlike an
item they have no stable name for a rule to attach to. Giving them one is the
whole job. The interface to hang it off has been there since 1.2.0.
