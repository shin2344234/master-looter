For Crimson Desert 2.01.00 (exe 1.0.0.2760).

Veins break the way a pickaxe breaks them, and Chinese, Japanese and Korean
menus render instead of showing boxes.

## Ore

1.3.0 sent the game a drop event at a vein. The contents spilled and were picked
up, but the node itself stayed in the world, so the same vein could be struck
again on a later pass and pay out twice.

The mod now drives the game's own state machine instead, firing the two events a
pickaxe fires: the swing landing, then the break. The node empties itself
through the game's own path and then disappears, which is what your own swing
does to it.

Because that replaced the old path rather than joining it, the three checkboxes
for breaking ore are one again. Both experiments are gone.

Known and not fixed. A vein pays either one or two ore, and which you get
depends on whether your character is carrying a mining bonus buff at that
moment: two of the node's three drop rows are gated on that buff. Mining by hand
behaves exactly the same way, so this is the game's own rule rather than
something the mod does to the vein, but it does mean the yield is not constant
and you will notice it changing during a run.

## Fixed

Ice walls and other puzzle prefabs are no longer struck. Five of them are tagged
as ore with a tag source, and the decision path was consulting its own shorter
list instead of deferring to the off-limits rule that already knew better.

Chinese, Japanese and Korean menus showed boxes instead of text. The glyph
ranges are now built from whichever translation is loaded and merged with a CJK
font, and the font atlas is rebuilt between frames rather than in the middle of
one, which is what made the old attempt crash.

A vein's own spill is exempt from the storage-heap rule for three metres and
forty-five seconds. Ore the mod had just knocked loose was being read as the
contents of a container and left on the ground.

## Also

Logs rotate. The last twelve sessions are kept and compressed, so a report can
include the run before the one that broke.

Crops have their own switch, separate from plants. Sweet potatoes and the like
were being taken by anyone who wanted herbs.
