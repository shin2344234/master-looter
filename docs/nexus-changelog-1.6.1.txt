For Crimson Desert 2.01.00 (exe 1.0.0.2760).

A wagon can no longer pass for your character.

## What was wrong

1.6.0 finds the body you are steering by the gear parented to it, which is how
auto-loot started working as Damiane and Oongka. It counted children raw. A
wagon carries about ten attached parts and a horse carries tack, so both
cleared the bar, and on the pass that happened to decide either could out-count
your character. It was survivable because a wagon and a horse are normally
parked beside you, so the scan still covered roughly the right ground. One that
drives off takes the scan with it, and a centre fifty metres away reaches
nothing useful.

## What changed

Equipment counts for more than cargo. A child that the scan has already
identified as an item is worth eight ordinary children, because a dressed
character carries items and a cart carries cart. That count is taken from work
the scan already does, so it costs nothing, and it only speaks for something
close enough for its children to have been identified, which is where the
confusion was. Your body seen from 1,700 metres still has no identified
children and is still chosen the old way, so nothing about the Damiane fix
moved.

Whatever the scan is centred on now has to be beaten rather than tied. A
challenger needs half again the score, two more outright, and it has to hold
that lead for two and a half seconds. The game hands the mod its list of
objects a few at a time, so one pass is a poor witness: the pass that
enumerates the wagon and not you reports the wagon as the only thing carrying
anything.

Switching character clears the record. The previous body's gear was otherwise
still on file and won the next pick outright.

Confirmed in a session that opened on a six-part prop with no equipment on it,
then corrected onto the body carrying ten children with four pieces of
equipment, and stayed there while walking.

## Also

The scan census in the debug log names the entity the position belongs to. It
used to print the player identity while standing on the body, which is the one
confusion the whole area exists to clear up.
