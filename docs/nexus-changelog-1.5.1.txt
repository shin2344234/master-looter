For Crimson Desert 2.01.00 (exe 1.0.0.2760).

Wells give up their water and keep their bucket.

## Wells

Turn the handle yourself and the mod lifts the water out of the bucket. The
bucket stays on the well and the handle stays in your hands.

Off by default, under Looting as "Draw water from wells".

This was asked for four days ago and answered "not possible" twice, both times
by me and both times wrong. What made it look impossible is that drawing water
raises no loot event at all. Every way the mod has of taking something is one
descriptor with a mode byte, and both modes empty the object and remove it: the
water arrived and the bucket vanished, twice.

What a hand draw raises instead is a run of state transitions, which the mod had
never been able to see because its event spy discards anything the world raises
about itself. With that lifted, one transition out of fifteen turned out to be
the one aimed at the bucket rather than the winch, and driving that single
transition hands over the water and leaves everything else alone.

The whole method is written up, including the parts that were wrong:
https://github.com/shin2344234/master-looter/blob/main/docs/ore-investigation/WELL.md

## Fixed

A stack of four landing counted as three pick-ups that failed. Mining a rock
spills four Stones, four pick-ups go out, and the bag reports one item type
going up by four. Only one of the four was credited and the other three aged out
as failures, which on a machine where the bag's own slot count cannot be read
could raise "bag full" in the middle of a run where everything was landing.

Standing next to a well armed seven parts of it every five seconds, for as long
as you stood there, at objects the mod had already decided it would not touch.
Arming asks the same question the verdict asks now.

## Also

The log says more about what it cannot explain. Live creatures are named whether
they get caught or not, whatever their category byte. Events the world raises
about itself are named rather than dropped, which is the blind spot that hid the
ore vein's own removal for days. A gimmick's state changes are logged with the
name of the transition where the game's own strings carry it. And an action you
take by hand that the mod cannot account for leaves a line instead of nothing.
