Test plan for 1.6.4

Two things changed since the last version of this plan. The Creatures tab is
gone, so the step about it is replaced. And the count of dropped work now
accumulates, so the world-change check will actually report something; the last
run showed nothing because of a fault in my logging, not because nothing
happened.

Start by confirming the log's first line:

    Master Looter v1.6.4 for Crimson Desert 2.01.00

The plugin should hash to a03f2f29... If you want to be certain it is the file
I packaged, that is the check.


BEFORE PLAYING

1. The hook lines.
   Near the top of the log, the three lines about Present, ResizeBuffers and
   SetColorSpace1 that say another mod detoured them first. Each should now end
   with where the jump lands:

       ..., into SOMETHING.asi at 00007FF...: another mod detoured it first

   Last time this read "into ? at ...", because the jump landed on a page the
   other mod allocated and the walk stopped there. It follows those now. If it
   still says "into an allocated stub at ...", the chain ends somewhere with no
   module attached and I want to see the line.

2. Language on a preset.
   Looting, Presets: save your current settings as "langtest". Then General,
   Language, click a different language and watch the menu change. Go back and
   load "langtest". The menu should return to English as the preset loads,
   without you touching a language button.


PLAYING

3. Ordinary looting.
   Ground items, an ore vein, an insect. The regression check that matters most,
   since the hook installer and the scan both changed underneath everything
   else. If loot stops arriving, stop there.

4. Mount and dismount a few times while looting is running.
   Easier than fast travelling and it trips the same purge, through the
   player-actor-changed trigger rather than the distance one. Do it near things
   actively being looted, then search the log for "dropped":

       [scan] paused: player actor changed (mount, cutscene or area); dropped N queued actions aimed at the old world

   A count above zero is the crash not happening. This is the one test that
   failed to report last time, so it is the one I most want a result from.

5. Leaving one species alone, from the Items tab.
   This replaces the Creatures tab step. Creatures you catch become items, and
   refusing the item refuses the catch, which has always been true.

   In the Looting tab, above the collection switches, there should now be a line
   saying so, and a warning in orange about the small flying insects. Both are
   new.

   Then: Items tab, search "iguana", set Item_Iguana to never, and go and stand
   next to an iguana with Small animals switched on. It should be left alone.
   The ini should gain a line in [Items], not [Creatures], which no longer
   exists.

   Do not try this with a butterfly or a cricket. The game gives the small
   flying insects no model, so the mod cannot tell them apart and no filter
   reaches them. That is what went wrong on your last run.

6. The owned goods key.
   General, Keys, "Take goods that belong to someone", unbound by default. The
   control is a Rebind button; press it and then press the key you want. Find
   something the log is refusing with "owned by someone (theft)" and press it:

       Master Looter: taking owned goods, the game calls this stealing

   The item should then be taken. Press again for "leaving owned goods alone".
   After quitting, [MasterLooter] in the ini should have KeyOwned set to a
   number.

7. Ore bonus during a well.
   Set "Extra ore per vein" to 2, turn OFF "Mine ore veins for you", turn ON
   "Draw water from wells". Find a well with a vein near it. Draw the water, and
   when the log says "[well] took from bucket", swing your own pickaxe at the
   vein within about two seconds. You should get your normal amount and no

       [ore] vein the mod broke paid

   line for it. Miss the window and the result means nothing.

8. Wells.
   Nothing to test. I changed the redraw cooldown from one second to fifteen
   after misreading your log, you told me the old pace was what you wanted, and
   it is back to one second. Confirm it feels the way it did before and that is
   the whole check.

   For the record on why I got it wrong: no [loot] lines appear for well water,
   because a well yields through a state transition rather than a loot event, so
   the log looked like twenty-five draws producing nothing.


IN THE FILES AFTERWARDS

9. MasterLooter.ini: [Items] should carry your iguana override as a numeric key,
   [MasterLooter] should have KeyOwned and PadOwned. There should be no
   [Creatures] section at all now.

10. The settings migration. Copy the ini somewhere safe, delete the line reading
    ConfigVersion=2, start the game, quit. The ini should say ConfigVersion=2
    again and the log should say it migrated. Start and quit once more: it must
    not migrate a second time, and MasterLooter.backups must not gain a second
    file ending -v1.

11. Faults. Search the log for "[fault]". Each should read "first chance", and
    the first should be preceded by a line saying most of them are the game
    probing memory on purpose. They are not the crash, and they get sent to me
    as though they were.


WHAT CANNOT BE TESTED HERE

The train that #36 was about. I cannot put you next to one, and a horse or a
wagon proves nothing since the old build handled those correctly.

The hook installer race. A few instructions wide and not triggerable on purpose.
The only evidence is unexplained crashes at world load stopping, which is not
evidence anyone can go and collect.

The memory cache. It only ever showed up as scan time. If you want a soft look,
note the "scan X.X ms" figure on the Nearby tab early and again an hour in.

Filtering a specific small flying insect. Not a test, a limitation: there is
nothing in the game's data to filter on.
